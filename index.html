§01

Signing In

The warehouse tablet app now signs in once before it can be used. This is the tablet's login — your personal PIN comes right after and identifies each scan.

Sign the tablet in

What it does
  • When the app opens it now shows a sign-in screen asking for a username and password. Signing in unlocks the app on that tablet for the rest of the session.
  • This replaces the old behavior where the app opened straight to work — the sign-in is what lets the app talk to Odoo securely.
How to use it
  • Enter the warehouse Odoo username and password for the tablet and press Sign In.
  • You won't be asked again until the session ends — see Staying Signed In (§03).
Good to know
  • This is the app/tablet login, not your personal PIN. After signing in you'll pick your name and enter your PIN (§02).
  • If you don't know the tablet's sign-in username or password, ask a manager — it's a normal Odoo login, set up once per tablet.
After this updateReload the tablet once (pull down to refresh, or reload the page) so it picks up the new version. If the app looks stuck or throws an error right after the update, a refresh clears it.

If sign-in won't go through

Good to know
  • A wrong username or password shows Invalid username or password — re-check both; passwords are case-sensitive.
  • Too many wrong tries in a row will temporarily lock sign-in for that location for a few minutes. If you see Too many login attempts, wait a few minutes and try again with the correct password.
§02

Your PIN

Once the tablet is signed in, each person enters their own 4-digit PIN. This stamps every scan — DoorDash, scrap, receiving, counts — with who did it.

Enter your PIN

How to use it
  • Pick your name, then type your 4-digit PIN. That's it — you're in.
  • Handing the tablet to someone else? Use Not you? Switch employee so their scans are recorded under their name.
Good to know
  • Your Employee record must have a PIN set. If you see No PIN set for this employee — ask a manager to set one in Employees, a manager needs to set your PIN before you can continue.
  • Managers set it at Employees → (the person) → HR Settings → PIN Code.
  • There's no "any PIN works" shortcut — a blank or wrong PIN is rejected. This is deliberate, so scans are always tied to a real person.
§03

Staying Signed In

A tablet sign-in lasts about half a day, then the app asks you to sign in again.

How long a sign-in lasts

What it does
  • A tablet stays signed in for about 12 hours. After that the app returns to the sign-in screen on its own.
Good to know
  • If the app suddenly shows the sign-in screen mid-shift, the session simply expired — sign in again and carry on. Nothing you scanned is lost.
  • Your PIN is separate from the tablet sign-in — after a re-sign-in you'll enter your PIN again too.
  • Want a longer (or shorter) window before re-sign-in? A manager can ask Brapps to adjust it.
§04

Cookie Batches

When you confirm a frozen cookie manufacturing order, Odoo automatically creates the matching Dough and Core batches underneath it, sized from the recipe.

Dough & Core auto-create from a cookie order

What it does
  • Confirming a frozen cookie Manufacturing Order automatically raises two linked orders — one for the Dough and one for the Core — for the exact amount that batch of cookies needs.
  • Each linked order lists the ingredient amounts to pull, straight from that recipe.
How to use it
  • Create and confirm the cookie order as usual — the Dough and Core orders appear on their own. Open them to see the ingredients for the run.
Good to know
  • Dough and Core amounts are driven by the recipe's batch size, measured in ounces. If a Dough or Core order ever calls for an amount that looks far too small or too large, its recipe's batch unit is the thing to check — flag it to Bryan and it can be corrected.
§05

KeHE EDI Orders

KeHE sends purchase orders to Fat & Weird electronically (EDI). Those orders appear in Odoo on their own, and as you work them, Odoo answers KeHE back electronically at each step — no manual uploads.

Orders from KeHE arrive on their own

What it does
  • When KeHE issues a purchase order over the EDI network, a matching sales order appears in Odoo automatically, carrying KeHE's PO number and requested delivery date.
  • As you process that order, KeHE is answered automatically: confirming it sends an order acknowledgment, validating the delivery sends a ship notice, and posting the invoice sends the invoice — each step leaves a "sent to SPS" note in the record's log.
How to use it
  • Nothing extra — confirm, ship, and invoice the order the way you always do. The electronic replies ride along on their own.

Which orders send EDI documents

What it does
  • Only orders that arrived through the EDI connection send documents back to KeHE. KeHE orders placed through the Shopify wholesale site are invoiced the normal way and send no EDI documents.
Good to know
  • KeHE rejects documents that are missing required details. For every product KeHE buys, the barcode (UPC) must be filled in on the product, and the KeHE orders need payment terms set — if KeHE reports a rejected document, those are the first things to check.
§06

Changelog

What changed in your Odoo customizations, newest first.

2026-08-13 · PENDING PRODUCTION PUSH

Shopify invoice accuracy, KeHE EDI & product setup fixes

  • Fixed: the Create New Product tool (Inventory app) was failing with a "can't find 'Regular Roll Film' product" error after the roll film products were renamed. The tool now finds the right roll film by its internal reference instead of its name, so renaming those products can't break it again.
  • Fixed: when a Shopify order shipped in more than one package, its discount, shipping, and tax charges could be billed in full on the first shipment instead of matching what had actually gone out — invoices now bill those charges in step with delivery.
  • Fixed: some orders were staying marked To Invoice in Odoo even after everything on them had genuinely been billed.
  • Fixed: a small number of orders were being charged sales tax twice, or picked up an extra packaging-box charge nobody ordered.
  • Fixed: electronic (EDI) documents meant for KeHE were also being sent for KeHE orders that actually came through the Shopify wholesale site — KeHE rejected them all. EDI replies now go out only for orders that arrived through the EDI connection (§05).
  • EDI ship notices and invoices now carry KeHE's location codes in the exact format KeHE requires, and delivery charges no longer appear as product lines on electronic invoices.
  • Currently in testing — this entry will move to a dated changelog line once verified and live.
2026-07-16

Correct Dough & Core amounts on cookie orders

  • Confirming certain frozen cookie orders was creating Dough and Core batches that called for far too little of each ingredient. Those recipes have been corrected and the amounts now match the recipe.
  • The step that sizes Dough and Core batches was also hardened so this can't happen again when a new cookie is set up.
2026-07-07

Sign-in hardening

  • Repeated wrong passwords now temporarily lock sign-in for that location for a few minutes — a guard against password guessing.
  • Behind the scenes: expired tablet sign-ins are cleaned up automatically, and warehouse actions now record which operator performed them.
2026-07-06

Warehouse app sign-in & PINs

  • The warehouse app now requires a one-time tablet sign-in (username + password) before use, and stays signed in for about 12 hours.
  • Each operator enters a 4-digit PIN so every scan is attributed to them.
  • Your PIN must be set on your Employee record — a missing PIN now shows a clear "ask a manager to set one" message instead of quietly letting anyone through.
  • Fixed: DoorDash, scrap, and receiving submissions that were failing with an "Authentication required" error.
